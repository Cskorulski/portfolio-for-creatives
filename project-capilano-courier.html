Capilano Courier

Art Direction . Production Management . Conceptual Illustration

The Capilano Courier is an independent, student-run periodical that showcases writing and artwork from the Capilano University student body.

I’ve worked for the Capilano courier for 3 years, beginning as a part-time illustrator in 2023. Over my university career, I’ve taken on a contract as crew illustrator, production manager, and art director. Each with a unique set of responsibilities including high-fidelity and high-volume conceptual illustration, production workflow refinement, image optimization, creative direction, and layout design.

Layout design, typography, & crew portraits by Cameron Skorulski
