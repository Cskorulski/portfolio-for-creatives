Fruition Movement

Illustration . Brand Identity . Print Design

Collaborators

Emilyn Lai . Layout & Brand Identity
Kayla Do . Brand Identity
Leonardo Velazquez Chazaro . UI / UX

Traditional gym culture is rife with competition, strict body ideals, and heteronormativity. Over the course of collecting primary research with queer and gender-diverse individuals, we identified key themes that contribute to gym intimidation (or “gym-timidation”). The most prominent being, fear of judgement, exclusion, and an overall lack of visible community.

We wondered... how can we make a gym for the gym-curious? For those who seek community... without prying eyes? For those who may be seeking a physique that affirms their gender... without forcing them into a box?

We devised Fruition Movement. A gym for all, but designed first and foremost with queer and trans-bodies in mind.
